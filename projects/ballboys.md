My CIS4930 class at UNF had a final (group, but I completed it alone) project of "a simple 2D game with a goal and reward."
So I made a 3D multiplayer (up to 10 per game) platformer with procedurally generated maps... and a cloud-stored cosmetics system with full RGB customization.
I did all of this within the 4-5 months of the class starting/ending... and I obtained approval for a Steam release. (first ever Steam application from me)
The Steam release has pulled over 300k impressions with a 4% Click-Thru-Rate in its first month. 
I placed top 3 in the UNF Computing Symposium with my presentation of the game: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4roU2igziK4
